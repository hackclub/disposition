# How will the shop work?

Basically, you will be able to buy any commercial CD, cassette or vinyl record from the shop. If the one you want is missing, you can submit a request to add it!
To submit a request you will need to also give us links to a local retailer in your region.

Keep in mind, not all physical releases of albums are available in all regions. I can give you a personal example (from me, the evil wizard that's writing the docs): you can't buy Chevelle CDs in Europe. it's that simple. Their label never released any of their music in Europe until very recently, which sucks, because Chevelle is a really good band and you should probably check them out if you're into hard rock or metal.
<br /> Consequently, some shop items won't be available in certain regions because of stuff like this :(

Anyways, here are the rates! Everything in the shop is priced in coins, and **1 dollar = 5 coins**.
You earn coins by shipping projects: every approved hour is worth **$5, so 25 coins**. Only the hours a reviewer approves count, so if they cut some of your time, you get paid for what's left.
